# 01 Product Overview

## Client
LamSparks Photography, Abuja. Owner Osemene. Name is always written **LamSparks** (capital L, capital S). "Lam" comes from Lametu, her mother. Instagram: @lamsparksphotography. Tagline: *Capturing Moments. Preserving Memories. Sparkling Lives.*

## Problem
Enquiries get lost in DMs. Deposits and preparation details are tracked from memory. Client care is manual. The website does not carry the photography.

## Four connected parts on one database
1. **Public website**: portfolio, stories, testimonials, session pages, About.
2. **Booking journey**: asks about the person first, shows a personal estimate, hands off to WhatsApp with answers attached.
3. **WhatsApp assistant**: routine messages from deposit to delivery. Hands anything personal to a human.
4. **Studio dashboard** (private): pipeline, client records, deposit queue, calendar, editing tracker, human inbox, content calendar, asset library.

Plus: Instagram and Facebook DM automation for first questions (Launch set, if confirmed), and background AI helpers (file 10).

## Goals the product must meet
- Show LamSparks at its best to a stranger on a phone within seconds.
- Tell Osemene's story and the name's meaning in her voice.
- Help visitors find their session type and understand inclusions before money is mentioned.
- Stop enquiries being lost; track deposits and prep in the system.
- Keep client care going without becoming a broadcast.
- Be owned by LamSparks: editable by her team, accounts and code in her name.

## Scope sets
**Launch (build now):** website (portfolio, testimonials, sessions, About, FAQ, Journal, Legal), booking journey, client records, full pipeline, deposit queue, calendar, editing and delivery tracker, content calendar with asset library, caption bank, posting reminders, human inbox, WhatsApp assistant (booking, deposits, prep, reminders, delivery), login and roles, editing training, IG/FB DM automation (welcome and options), two background AI features (inbox triage, deposit screenshot checking).

**Follow-on (stub only):** client care calendar (birthdays, anniversaries, seasonal), referral programme, review requests, online deposit payment (Paystack or Flutterwave), fuller reports, existing client import with opt-in, remaining AI features.

**Growth (do not build):** campaigns, automated posting, richer assistant steps, print and album sales, gift vouchers.

**Never build:** client logins, open-ended AI chat with clients, TikTok DM automation, accounting or tax reporting, any unofficial WhatsApp connection (risks number ban).

## Hard rules
1. The assistant never confirms a deposit. A team member checks it against the bank and records it.
2. Posting stays a human action. The system only reminds.
3. AI never speaks to clients as an AI and never sends without review, unless explicitly enabled per message type.
4. Consent is enforced in code: no marketing image, testimonial or greeting reaches anyone without recorded consent.
5. The agreed price always matches the answers. Changing an answer clears the agreement tick.
6. Every account lives in LamSparks' name. Builder has access, not ownership.
7. Existing clients receive no automated messages until they opt in.

## Users
- **Visitor/client:** no login. Uses website, booking page, WhatsApp, Instagram/Facebook DMs.
- **Owner (Osemene):** full access, approves wording, sets targets and prices.
- **Team member:** limited access per role (file 09).
